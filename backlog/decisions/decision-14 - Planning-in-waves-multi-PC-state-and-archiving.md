## Context

Owner decisions of 2026-11-06.

## Decision

Future GOALs carry only outcome and criteria; the architect details tasks wave by wave when the GOAL starts, referencing symbols, not line numbers; implementers preflight the spec against current code. Tracker, decisions, reports, handoff and events are in git; locks, claims, heartbeats, caches and worktrees are not. A pause skill brings everything to a clean committed point (WIP branches); push only with owner authorization; resume works after a clone. Closed GOALs and tasks are archived, never deleted; PLAN.md stays short.

## Consequences

None beyond the decision itself.
