## Context

Option B chosen by the owner on 2026-11-06 among: no measurement (A), light measurement (B), full evaluation harness first (C).

## Decision

The plugin records per task: tokens per model, wall time, owner interventions and rework. The Agriturismo 7C pilot is compared with 7B under the old rules. A full evaluation harness is a later GOAL (M-5, proposed).

## Consequences

Measurement fields are part of the M-2 event log design.
