## Context

Q9.

## Decision

A fresh Opus reviewer runs every 15 integrated tasks or at GOAL end, whichever comes first, on a commit range between tags. Findings: violation (priority task), debt (queued), suggestion (noted).

## Consequences

Review does not block work in progress.
