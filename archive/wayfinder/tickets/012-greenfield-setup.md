## Question

On a greenfield repo, which standards does maestro propose and scaffold, and
how much does it ask versus default? Candidates: the tracker (GitHub or GitLab
issues when a remote exists, Backlog.md or a local file otherwise), goal and
milestone conventions, labels and the task state machine, a `CLAUDE.md`
skeleton, the binding itself. Prefer official CLIs and initializers where they
exist. Decide the interview shape and what the first goal looks like right
after setup.
